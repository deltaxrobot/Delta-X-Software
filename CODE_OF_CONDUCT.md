# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, professional, and technically constructive
community. Contributors must welcome people regardless of background, experience,
identity, ability, nationality, or personal characteristics.

Expected behavior includes:

- discussing ideas and evidence rather than attacking people;
- giving actionable, patient review feedback;
- acknowledging safety, security, and hardware limitations honestly;
- respecting privacy and confidential machine/customer information;
- accepting maintainer decisions and correcting harmful behavior promptly.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate
sabotage, or repeated bad-faith disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
private contact or security-reporting channel. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently
ban participants when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical and conflicts of interest should be disclosed.

This policy applies in repository discussions, code review, community spaces, and
public representation of the project.
